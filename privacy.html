Our applications do not collect, record, process, store, sell, or transfer any personally identifiable information (such as full name, phone number, email address, contact list, photos, or personal files). No user account creation, profile setup, or login is required to access our software.

2. Location Permission & Strict On-Device Processing

For Simple Prayer Times & Widget, location permissions (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) are requested strictly and solely to compute precise local astronomical prayer times and determine Qibla direction based on your geographic coordinates.

Core Privacy Guarantee: All location computations are performed entirely on-device. Your coordinates are never uploaded, logged, or transmitted to our servers or any external parties.
