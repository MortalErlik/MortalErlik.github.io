Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026 | Coral Studios ("We", "Our") deeply respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the data practices of all Android applications developed and published by Coral Studios.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Our applications (including Simple Prayer Times & Widget and upcoming studio releases) do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, personal files, or contact lists). No user account creation or registration is required to use our software.

2. Location Permission & On-Device Processing

Location permission requested by Simple Prayer Times & Widget is used strictly and solely to calculate accurate local astronomical prayer times and Qibla direction based on your coordinates. Your location is computed strictly on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or any external third parties.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep our free applications available, we integrate Google AdMob as our advertising provider. AdMob may process anonymous technical identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in accordance with applicable privacy regulations and user consent choices to display personalized or non-personalized ads. For details on how Google processes ad data, please refer to Google's Advertising Policies.

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Any premium upgrades, customizations, or ad-removal purchases are processed exclusively via official Google Play Billing. We never receive, process, or store your credit card details or financial information; all transactions are handled securely by Google.
